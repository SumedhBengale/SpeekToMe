FAQ

Pricing questions.

How does billing work? +
You're billed $2 per month from the date you subscribe. Your 50-hour usage resets at the start of each billing cycle. You can cancel anytime from the dashboard — no contracts, no lock-in.
Can I get a refund? +
Yes! We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied with speekto.me Pro within the first 7 days of your subscription, contact us for a full refund. See our Refund Policy for details.
What happens after 50 hours? +
Your dictation services pause until your next billing cycle starts. We'll alert you on the desktop app when you're approaching your limit. For most users, 50 hours is more than enough — that's over 1.5 hours of dictation every day.
Is there a free plan? +
Yes! The free tier gives you basic transcription with a 5-minute per session limit. It's perfect for trying out speekto.me before committing to Pro. No credit card required.
Do you offer team or enterprise plans? +
Not yet, but we're working on it! If you need custom volume, team management, or enterprise features, reach out to us at contact@synaptican.com and we'll work something out.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept all major credit and debit cards, including Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. Payments are securely processed through our billing partner.
