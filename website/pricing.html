FAQ

Pricing questions.

How does billing work? +
You're billed $2 per month from the date you subscribe. You get unlimited AI voice transcription with no hidden fees or daily limits. You can cancel anytime directly from your dashboard — no contracts, no lock-in.
Can I get a refund? +
Yes! We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied with SpeekTo.Me Pro within the first 7 days of your subscription, contact us for a full refund. See our Refund Policy for details.
Is transcription really unlimited on Pro? +
Yes! SpeekTo.Me Pro includes unlimited AI audio transcription without daily session caps. To safeguard our high-performance infrastructure and maintain fast response times for all users, standard fair-use guidelines apply, but normal human dictation has no cap.
Is there a free plan? +
Yes! The Starter tier gives you 1 hour per month of basic transcription with no daily usage caps. It's perfect for testing out SpeekTo.Me before upgrading to Pro. No credit card required.
Do you offer team or enterprise plans? +
Not yet, but we're working on it! If you need custom volume, team management, or enterprise features, reach out to us at contact@synaptican.com and we'll work something out.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept all major credit and debit cards, including Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. Payments are securely processed through our billing partner.
