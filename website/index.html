FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about limits, privacy, or setup? We have answers.

How is the transcription latency so low? +
By downmixing audio locally to 16kHz mono and encoding to Opus, we reduce transmission payloads by 99%. Small payloads travel faster over networks. Combined with high-speed cloud transcription infrastructure, transcription completes almost instantly.
What happens if I reach the 50-hour monthly limit? +
Your dictation services will temporarily pause until the start of your next billing cycle. We will alert you on the desktop Account panel if you are nearing your limit. If you need more hours, you can contact us for custom plans.
Is my voice recording data stored permanently? +
No. We process all audio memory buffers ephemerally. Once our transcription engine finishes translating the Ogg/Opus container to text, the audio clip is immediately deleted from our servers. We never train models on your data.
Can I customize how the AI formats my voice? +
Yes. Inside the client dashboard, you can define custom formatting prompts to instruct the AI (e.g. "Write as Python code comments" or "Keep it in bullet points"). You can create multiple presets and switch between them.
Which languages are supported? +
Our transcription engine supports 99+ languages. speekto.me inherits this multilingual capability automatically. You can dictate in English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, and many more languages — no configuration needed.
Do I need an internet connection? +
Yes, an active internet connection is required. speekto.me streams compressed audio to our cloud API for transcription and formatting. However, the payload is extremely small (161KB for 60s of audio) so even slow mobile connections work well.
