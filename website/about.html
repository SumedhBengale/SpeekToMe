About Us

At speekto.me, our mission is to eliminate typing friction completely. Typing limits our speed of thought. We built a solution that lets you speak naturally and see your thoughts instantly translated into polished, grammatically correct text inside any input field.

Our Technology

Our desktop client captures voice input, downmixes it to 16,000 Hz mono, and compresses it using the Opus audio codec—reducing bandwidth consumption by 99% (converting 15.4 MB of raw WAV audio into a tiny 161 KB Ogg stream). The server parses the Ogg header positions instantly, forwards it to Groq's Whisper v3 Turbo model for high-speed transcription (under 450ms), and applies grammar formatting via DeepSeek LLM models.
