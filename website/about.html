About Us

We're building the voice layer for your OS.

At SpeekTo.Me, our mission is to eliminate typing friction completely. Typing limits your speed of thought. We built a solution that lets you speak naturally and see your thoughts instantly translated into polished text.

We believe the keyboard is a bottleneck. Your thoughts move at the speed of speech — your tools should keep up.

Principles

What we stand for.

Every decision we make is guided by three core principles.

Speed Above All

We obsess over milliseconds. Every audio sample, every network packet, every API call is optimized to be as fast as physically possible.

Privacy First

Your voice data is processed ephemerally and never stored. We don't train on your data. Your conversations remain yours.

Invisible Design

The best tool is one you don't notice. SpeekTo.Me runs silently in the background, appearing only when you need it.

Philosophy

Technology should be invisible.

We believe the best software disappears into your workflow. SpeekTo.Me doesn't ask you to switch apps, learn new interfaces, or change how you work. It simply listens when you speak and types when you stop.

Our client is built on Tauri (Rust + WebView), ensuring minimal memory footprint and lightning-fast startup times. No Electron bloat, no heavyweight frameworks — just pure performance.
